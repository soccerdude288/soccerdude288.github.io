Kroger MCP

Grocery shopping from a chat

My fork of the open-source kroger-mcp server, which lets an AI assistant search Kroger and fill a cart. I added live prices and stock to the cart, unit-price math for bulk and by-weight items, and tools that suggest cheaper or in-stock swaps. I also fixed token handling that was costing three or four extra API calls per request.
